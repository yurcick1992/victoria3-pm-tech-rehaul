Ladder check — does the tech ladder hold? — the acceptance criterion (BALANCE_FRAMEWORK §10.11), live on the scenario below. Three faults per industry, summing: the best tier present loses money; a tier two or more eras behind it still turns a profit; the ladder is inverted (the best tier earns less than the one below it). Counted only on buildings the scenario actually contains — an industry with no buildings, or a tier whose Number is 0, is never a fault and is never the comparison partner for one, so zeroing things out can only ever lower this count. Shipyards and art academies are reported but excused (both carry targets they cannot meet by construction).
Scenario panel — buy/sell orders & prices — building inputs = BUY orders, outputs = SELL orders (each building's goods × its Number). A preset fills the whole scenario from one country's vanilla 1836 market (buildings, PMs, treaty transfers, pops, base wage); pops buy orders are derived from those pops and their SoL (plus the baskets buildings buy for their slaves — hover for that part), and trade is yours to dial in. The price column's toggle switches between locked (edit each % by hand) and auto (recomputed live from orders: price = base × [1 + 0.75·clamp((buy−sell)/min(buy,sell), ±1)], propagated to every building). Click a section header to fold it. Hover an industry below to flag its goods here.
