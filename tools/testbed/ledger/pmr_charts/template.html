PMR, vanilla and No Free Upgrades: a century each, run by run

Victoria 3 version 1.15.1 played from 1836 to 1936 as observer games (every country run by the AI, no player), under three setups. Each thin line is one whole game; each bold line is the median of that setup's games in each year.

How to read it. The axes are fixed: switching a setup or a kind of line off never rescales a chart. Hover or tap a chart for the medians of one year.

Terms. GDP is the figure the game displays. Unemployment including peasants (U*) is the share of the workforce that is unemployed or lives by subsistence farming: the lower it is, the further a country has moved its people into paid work. Productive workers per capita (W) counts salaried workers outside government and the military, per head of population. The investment pool is the private capital waiting to be spent on construction, shown in years of GDP: a pool that keeps growing is capital nobody finds a use for. The shortlist is Great Britain, the USA, France, the Netherlands and Belgium (or the United Netherlands) and Prussia / North Germany / Germany, read as one economy. A major's pool is left out in years when the country has under 5 million people.

Which games. Vanilla and No Free Upgrades also count the ten games each of an earlier batch on the same game version (12–14 November 2026); play speed is shown only for the games of this batch, which alternate on one machine over the same days. A country's GDP moves with its territory from game to game; its shares (U*, the pool in years of GDP) compare better.
