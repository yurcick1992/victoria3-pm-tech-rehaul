Ladder
Ladder check — does the tech ladder hold? — the acceptance criterion (BALANCE_FRAMEWORK §10.11), live on the scenario below. Three faults per industry, summing: the best tier present loses money; a tier two or more eras behind it still turns a profit; the ladder is inverted (the best tier earns less than the one below it). Counted only on buildings the scenario actually contains — an industry with no buildings, or a tier whose Number is 0, is never a fault and is never the comparison partner for one, so zeroing things out can only ever lower this count. Shipyards and art academies are reported but excused (both carry targets they cannot meet by construction).
Scenario panel — buy/sell orders & prices — building inputs = BUY orders, outputs = SELL orders (each building's goods × its Number). A preset fills the whole scenario from one country's vanilla 1836 market (buildings, PMs, treaty transfers, pops, base wage); pops buy orders are derived from those pops and their SoL (plus the baskets buildings buy for their slaves — hover for that part), and trade is yours to dial in. The price column's toggle switches between locked (edit each % by hand) and auto (recomputed live from orders: price = base × [1 + 0.75·clamp((buy−sell)/min(buy,sell), ±1)], propagated to every building). Click a section header to fold it. Hover an industry below to flag its goods here. A design choice worth knowing: services, transportation and electricity are local goods — in the game a state's substitution supply is its own production plus (1 − its GDP share) × 0.25 of the market's, so a pop never sees the whole market's stock of one. This sheet is a single state, and treating it as the whole market is the one case where that rule does nothing — so it models a representative state instead: a fifth of the country's GDP holding a fifth of the local supply, ⇒ ×0.40 on those three goods' availability. It changes how each need splits, never how much money a need has, so whatever a local good gives up its competitors gain. Measured against eight vanilla savegames' own stored purchase weights it cuts the error on the affected needs from 4.23 to 0.84 points; hover a local good's pops cell for the arithmetic. Revertible in the model as LOCAL_MULT = 1.
